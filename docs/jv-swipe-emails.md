## EMAIL 1 — Brian Tracy's list

**Angle:** thirty years of expertise, finally named and made sellable.

### Subject line options

1. You have a methodology. You have just never named it.
2. The thirty years nobody can see
3. What happens to everything you know?

*Recommended: #1. Specific, flattering, and it creates an itch — "wait, do I?"*

**Preview text:** Most experts never name the thing they are actually best at.

### Body

I want to tell you something I have believed for a very long time.

Every person who has been good at something for thirty years has a method. A real one.
A sequence of moves they run without having to think about it anymore.

They have just never written it down.

I have watched this my entire career. I will sit down with a man who has been selling
for twenty-five years, and inside of ten minutes he hands me four or five principles
that cost him a fortune in mistakes to learn. Principles worth more than most of what
sits on the business bestseller list.

And then he goes home. And it stays in his head.

Here is what bothers me about that.

That knowledge does not compound. It does not get taught. It cannot be licensed, or
packaged, or sold, or left to anyone. And one day it simply goes away.

Now — I know exactly why it stays in there. It is not laziness. The people I am
describing are the least lazy people I know.

It is that *writing* is a completely different skill from *knowing.* You can be the
best in your city at what you do and still sit in front of a blank page for an hour
and produce nothing worth keeping. I have seen it a hundred times. I have felt it
myself.

So the problem was never the knowledge. The problem was always getting it out.

That is why I want you to see what [LANNY] has built.

It is not a writing course. There is nothing to study, and there is no blank page
anywhere in it.

You talk. That is the whole thing.

An AI interviewer asks you one question at a time — the way a serious ghostwriter
would, the kind who charges forty thousand dollars and takes nine months — and it
keeps asking until what is in your head is out on the table where you can see it.

Then it hands it back to you. Named. Organized. In your own words, not somebody
else's template.

Your methodology. With a name on it.

I am hosting a live session with [LANNY] on [DATE] at [TIME] to show you exactly how
this works. Not a demo of software. He is going to take a real person through it, live,
and you will watch a methodology come out of somebody's head in front of you.

Register here: [REGISTRATION LINK]

If you have spent decades getting good at something and never turned it into anything
you can hand to another person — this is the hour that fixes it.

Brian Tracy

## EMAIL 2 — Jack Canfield's list

**Angle:** the book you have meant to write for twenty years, talked out in one weekend.

### Subject line options

1. The book you have been meaning to write
2. Twenty years of "someday"
3. You do not have writer's block. You have something else.

*Recommended: #3. It reframes their private shame and promises relief — strong open.*

**Preview text:** After thirty years of teaching people to write books, I finally
understand what actually stops them.

### Body

I have spent more than thirty years helping people get their message into the world.

In that time I have heard the same sentence more often than any other. You may have
said it yourself.

*"I have a book in me. I just have not written it yet."*

Sometimes it has been ten years. Sometimes twenty. I have met people in their seventies
still carrying a book they have never put down on paper.

And for a long time I thought the answer was discipline. Get up earlier. Write a
thousand words a day. Push through.

I do not think that anymore.

Because the people saying this sentence to me are not undisciplined. They have raised
children, built practices, survived things that would have flattened most people. They
have plenty of discipline.

What they have is something else entirely.

They can tell you their whole message out loud, beautifully, for two hours over dinner.
And then they sit down in front of a blank screen — and it all goes.

That is not writer's block. That is a translation problem. Everything they have is in
their *voice*, and we keep asking them to produce it in *type*.

So what if we simply stopped asking?

That is why I want you to meet [LANNY] and see what he has built.

You talk. You do not write and you do not type. An AI ghostwriter interviews you one
question at a time — patiently, the way a real one would, following what you say and
asking the next thing — until your whole message is out.

And then it gives it back to you as a manuscript. In your own voice. Your stories,
your phrasing, the way you actually talk.

Not a template. Not a fill-in-the-blank. Yours.

I am hosting a live session with him on [DATE] at [TIME]. He is going to take someone
through it in real time, and you are going to watch a book start coming out of a
person who has been carrying it around for twenty years.

Save your seat here: [REGISTRATION LINK]

If you have been meaning to write it since before your children were grown — come and
see this. It may be a great deal closer than you think.

With love,
Jack Canfield

**P.S.** The hardest part of writing a book was never the writing. It was getting what
is inside you to hold still long enough to look at it. That is exactly the part this
solves — and I want you to see it happen live on [DATE]. [REGISTRATION LINK]
